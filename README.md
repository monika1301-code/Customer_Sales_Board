# 📊 Executive Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Executive Sales Board is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, and gross profit across countries, sales channels, and product categories.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and gross profit.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Identify the top 5 and bottom 5 countries by revenue.
- Analyze sales channel performance.
- Evaluate product category revenue.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Sales Quantity
- Product Metrics

### Sales Analysis
- Monthly Revenue and Gross Profit
- Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Bottom 5 Countries by Revenue
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Revenue by Product Category

### Interactive Filters
- Year
- Month
- Product Category
- Country
